Give `wayfinder-tracer` a way off the map. A merged tracer that leaves only repetition behind (the second caller, the other six endpoints) now **graduates** that chunk to the normal build chain instead of earning further tracers: `to-spec-with-evidence` over what the tracers proved, `to-tickets` to slice it, fanned out one subagent per unblocked ticket, with a single line on the map pointing at the spec. The gate is a merged tracer rather than agreement reached in a grilling, and every graduated ticket has to widen a path a tracer already walked, so a ticket that opens a new path stays on the map. The rule goes into the doctrine as well as the skill, so plain `wayfinder` sessions on the same map inherit it.

A tracer ticket is now explicitly a question rather than a design: it states what the slice must reach end to end and what evidence it must bring back, and leaves module, library and arrangement to the session that picks it up, which grills them against the code as it stands then. That makes a tracer HITL to settle and AFK to build, and it makes `grill-with-docs` the skill that opens one.
